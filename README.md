# Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard
### Excel | Pivot Tables | Slicers | Data Cleaning

## Project Overview
Interactive sales and profitability dashboard built in Excel analyzing 
9,994 records from the Superstore dataset across 4 regions, 3 categories 
and 3 customer segments covering 2014–2017.

## Business Problem
How can we identify which regions, categories and discount tiers 
are driving or destroying profitability — and what actions should 
the business take?

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Cleaning and Calculated Columns

## Dashboard Features
- 4 interactive slicers controlling 6 charts simultaneously
- 5 KPI cards updating in real time
- 6 pivot driven charts covering region, category, 
  discount, margin, trend and sub-category analysis

## Key Findings
- Orders with no discount generated $320K profit while 
  high discount orders caused a $76K loss
- Discounts above 20% actively destroy margin
- Technology leads profit margin at 15.6% vs 
  Furniture at only 3.9%
- Tables sub-category alone lost $17,725 — 
  primary drag on Furniture performance
- Q4 consistently drives peak sales across all years

## Recommendation
Cap discounts at 20% across all categories. 
Conduct pricing strategy review on Tables sub-category. 
Prioritise Technology category for growth investment.
